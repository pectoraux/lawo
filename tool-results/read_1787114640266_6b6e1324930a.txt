    66→- a time

  1083→──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  1084→

  1087→I1.
  1088→Core remains domain-agnostic.
  1089→
  1090→I2.
  1091→Country-specific logic lives in packages.
  1092→
  1093→I3.

  1095→
  1096→I4.
  1097→Situation-specific logic lives in situation/procedure packages.
  1098→

  1100→LLM output is never authoritative legal truth.
  1101→
  1102→I6.
  1103→Every material decision has provenance.
  1104→
  1105→I7.
  1106→Every rule has temporal/version metadata.
  1107→
  1108→I8.
  1109→Community observations cannot masquerade as authority.
  1110→
  1111→I9.
  1112→Private tenant data cannot enter global knowledge without explicit,
  1113→authorized publication.
  1114→
  1115→I10.
  1116→Packages are independently versioned and deployable.
  1117→
  1118→I11.
  1119→Packages cannot silently mutate kernel semantics.
  1120→
  1121→I12.
  1122→Extensions cannot bypass capability permissions.
  1123→
  1124→I13.
  1125→Historical decisions remain reproducible.
  1126→
  1127→I14.
  1128→Production changes must preserve backward-compatible contracts unless
  1129→explicitly versioned.
  1130→
  1131→I15.
  1132→Architecture is changed only through an Architecture Change Order.
  1133→
  1134→I16.
  1135→No feature may introduce a new architectural primitive merely because it
  1136→makes one feature easier.
  1137→
  1138→I17.
  1139→Repeated code across verticals is evidence to improve the kernel or create a
  1140→shared capability, not evidence to duplicate vertical logic.
  1141→
  1142→I18.
  1143→A hardening sprint may improve implementation but may not redefine architecture.
